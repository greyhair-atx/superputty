they are not installed with SuperPuTTY.

EXTERNAL APPLICATIONS
---------------------

SuperPuTTY can start and host separately installed applications such as PuTTY
(and PSCP), KiTTY, mintty/Cygterm, VNC clients, FreeRDP or Windows Remote
Desktop, FileZilla, and WinSCP. These programs are not included in the
SuperPuTTY runtime or installers and are governed by their own licenses.
