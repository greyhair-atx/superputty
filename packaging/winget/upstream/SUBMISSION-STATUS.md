This is an experimental client result, not a pass on released WinGet or Microsoft's publishing/preindexed catalog.

No experimental MSI or alternative installer is part of this submission.

The tested 1.8.0-to-1.8.3 migration is documented at https://github.com/greyhair-atx/superputty/blob/c9330352ae09ee31d59ced5f14968ba5fb649e28/packaging/winget/CURRENT-USER-MIGRATION.md.
